# Reflection: The Mythical Man-Month — Frederick Brooks

**Read:** 2026-07-02
**Impact:** Why software projects fail, and the nature of编程 work.

## Core Insights

### 1. Brooks's Law
"Adding manpower to a late software project makes it later." Communication overhead scales as n². Some work cannot be parallelized.

### 2. The Tar Pit
Large-system programming is like a tar pit — you can escape small tasks, but large projects pull you deeper. The difficulty grows faster than the size.

### 3. No Silver Bullet
There is no single technique that will give an order-of-magnitude improvement in software productivity. Complexity is inherent, not accidental.

### 4. The Second System Effect
The second system is the most dangerous — it's where you add everything you wanted in the first one. Over-design eats projects.

### 5. The Mythical Man-Month
Time and people are not fungible. A project that takes one person 12 months cannot be done by 12 people in one month. Some tasks are inherently sequential.

## Principle Extracted
**Time and people are not interchangeable.** The essence of programming is building conceptual structures — and that takes time, not just people.
